Safety, requirements & measurements

Know the limits before you ride

Every rental runs on a few clear rules: who can ride, what fits, and how we keep a trail full of quads safe. Check your measurements now and you'll walk straight to your vehicle on the day.

What you need to ride

Have these ready at the trailhead desk. We check them before your walkthrough, so being prepared keeps you on the trail longer.

01

A valid driver's license

Bring the original. It's required for every rental. Dirt bikes are for riders who already have experience on a geared bike.

02

Age 18 or older

Riders must be 18 or older to rent a vehicle on their own. Younger riders can join as a passenger on a UTV with a parent or guardian.

03

A signed waiver

Everyone on a vehicle signs at the trailhead desk, passengers included. A parent or guardian signs for anyone under 18.

04

Your booking confirmation

Show the confirmation we sent when you booked, on your phone or printed. It tells us your vehicle, slot and group size.

05

A clear head

No alcohol or drugs before or during your ride. Our crew can turn away anyone they don't think is fit to ride.

06

Closed shoes and long pants

Boots or shoes that cover your ankles, plus long trousers. Sandals and shorts aren't safe on a trail.

Speed, spacing and group size

Limits are set trail by trail so your pace matches the ground. Keep to walking pace, 10 km/h, in the yard and staging area.

Speed, spacing and group limits by trail

Speed limits are maximums. Slow down whenever the ground, weather or dust calls for it, and widen the gap when the rider ahead kicks up dust.

The trail code

Six habits every rider follows, on every trail.

01

Stay on the marked trail

Cutting your own line damages the ridge and puts you where our guides can't see you.

02

Hold your gap

Keep at least the gap listed for your trail. Dust hides what's ahead, and a quad can't stop on a dime.

03

Helmet on, strap fastened

From the moment the engine starts until it's off. Goggles and gloves stay on too.

04

One rider per seat

Never carry more people than the vehicle has seats. Passengers stay seated and belted, or holding on where there's a grab bar.

05

Take crossings one at a time

Let the rider ahead clear a water crossing or rock ledge before you start. Go slow and steady, without stopping mid-way.

06

Stop when the crew says stop

If a guide or trail marshal signals you to stop, do it straight away. They can see what you can't.

Gear: what we give you, what you wear

Every rental includes a helmet, goggles and gloves in your size at no extra cost. Bringing your own? A DOT-approved helmet is welcome.

We provide

  • A fitted helmet Sized to your head using the chart above, and checked again at the trailhead.
  • Goggles To keep dust, grit and spray out of your eyes.
  • Gloves For grip on the handlebars and protection if you take a knock.

You wear or bring

  • Closed shoes or boots Ankle cover and a grippy sole for the foot pegs and floorboards.
  • Long trousers and sleeves Light, snug layers protect against scrapes, sun and flying gravel. Avoid loose scarves and dangling straps.
  • Water and sunscreen Trails run one to three hours in open sun. Leave valuables at the desk.

If something goes wrong

Stay calm and get help moving. Every trail is marked, so the crew can find you quickly.

+91 90000 11111 Trailhead desk 112 Emergency services
  1. Stop and switch off the engine Bring the vehicle to a halt on level ground if you can, and turn the engine off before you get off.
  2. Call for help Ring the trailhead desk. If someone is badly hurt, call 112 first, then the desk.
  3. Stay put with your group Don't move an injured rider unless they're in immediate danger. Wait for the crew to reach you.

Questions about the rules

What if I'm just under the height or weight limit?
The limits come from each vehicle's seat, belt and control reach, so we can't bend them. If you're close, call the trailhead desk. We can often suggest a different vehicle, or a guided UTV ride as a passenger.
Can I ride if I've had a drink?
No. There's no alcohol or drugs before or during your ride, and the crew can turn away anyone who isn't fit to ride. If that affects your booking, call us and we'll talk through your options.
Do passengers need to sign the waiver?
Yes. Everyone on a vehicle signs at the trailhead desk. A parent or guardian signs for anyone under 18.
What if I don't feel comfortable after the walkthrough?
Say so. The walkthrough on flat ground is there to catch exactly that. We'll move you to an easier vehicle or trail, or pair you with a guide.

Not sure you meet the limits? Just ask.

Call the trailhead desk and we'll check the numbers with you before you book.
