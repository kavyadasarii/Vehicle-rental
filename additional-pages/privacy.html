TRAILRIDGE
ATV & Off-Road Rentals

This Privacy Policy explains what information Trail Ridge Off-Road collects when you use our website, book a rental or guided tour, or create an account, and how that information is used, shared, and protected.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly, and some information automatically:

  • Account & booking details: name, email, phone number, and, at the trailhead, driver's license details required to operate a rental vehicle.
  • Payment information: processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers on our own servers.
  • Waiver & safety records: signed liability waivers and any medical or fitness-to-ride notes you choose to share with trailhead staff.
  • Usage data: pages visited, device and browser type, and approximate location, collected automatically via cookies and similar technology.
  • Communications: messages you send us by email, phone, or contact form.

2. How we use it

  • To process bookings, payments, and rental agreements.
  • To confirm eligibility (age, license) before issuing a vehicle.
  • To send booking confirmations, trail-safety updates, and, if you opt in, promotional offers.
  • To improve the website and our fleet, trail, and tour offerings.
  • To meet legal, insurance, and safety-record-keeping obligations.

3. Sharing & disclosure

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with: payment processors, to complete transactions; insurance providers, in connection with a claim or incident; guides and trailhead staff, to run your booking safely; and government or law-enforcement bodies where required by law.

4. Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in, remember your display preferences (like light/dark mode and text direction), and understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; some features, like staying logged in, may not work without them.

5. Data retention

Account and booking data is kept for as long as your account is active. Signed waivers and incident records are retained for the period required by our insurance policy and Indian law, typically several years, after which they're securely deleted.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures — encrypted connections, access controls, and regular review of who can see rider data — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending marketing communications, at any time by emailing ride@trailridgeoffroad.in. We may need to keep certain records, such as signed waivers, where we're legally required to.

8. Children's data

Riders under 18 may only be booked by a parent or legal guardian, who provides and controls any information about the minor. We don't knowingly collect information directly from children without a guardian's involvement.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services change. Material changes will be noted on this page with a new "last updated" date; continued use of the Services after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to ride@trailridgeoffroad.in or +91 90000 11111, or in person at the Shamirpet Trailhead, Hyderabad.
