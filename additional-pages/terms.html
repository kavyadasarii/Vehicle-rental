1. Eligibility & licensing

You must be at least 18 years old to book a rental or sign a rental agreement. Riders aged 12–17 may ride as passengers on a UTV or as guided-tour participants only, with a parent or legal guardian present and a signed minor waiver on file.

  • A valid government-issued driver's license is required to operate any rental vehicle.
  • No prior ATV, UTV or dirt bike experience is required — every rental starts with a guided walkthrough on flat ground.
  • We reserve the right to refuse a rental to anyone who appears intoxicated, medically unfit to ride, or unable to demonstrate basic control of the vehicle during the walkthrough.

2. Bookings & payment

Rentals and tours can be booked online, by phone, or in person at the Shamirpet trailhead. Full payment, or the deposit stated at checkout, is due at the time of booking to hold your vehicle or tour slot. Prices are quoted in Indian Rupees and include a basic damage waiver unless stated otherwise; taxes are added at checkout.

3. Cancellations & refunds

  • 24–48 hours before: 50% refund, or a full-value credit toward a future booking.
  • Under 24 hours, or a no-show: non-refundable, except where we cancel for weather or safety reasons.
  • We may cancel or reschedule a rental or tour due to unsafe trail conditions, extreme weather, or vehicle maintenance; in that case you'll receive a full refund or a free reschedule, your choice.

4. Rental conduct

You agree, for the full duration of a rental, to:

  • Wear the helmet and safety gear provided at all times while the vehicle is in motion.
  • Stay on marked trails and obey posted difficulty ratings and trail-crew instructions.
  • Not operate the vehicle under the influence of alcohol or drugs, or allow anyone not listed on the rental agreement to drive.
  • Return the vehicle at the agreed time and location, in the condition it was issued in, ordinary wear excepted.
  • Report any mechanical issue, warning light, or incident to trailhead staff immediately.

5. Damage, loss & late returns

The damage waiver included with your rental covers ordinary wear and accidental damage from normal riding within marked trails. It does not cover damage from reckless operation, riding outside marked trails, driving under the influence, or loss/theft caused by leaving the vehicle unsecured. Costs outside the waiver are charged at parts-and-labor cost, itemized on request. Vehicles returned more than 30 minutes late are billed for an additional rental period unless you've arranged an extension with trailhead staff in advance.

Riders must stay with the group and follow the guide's instructions; a guide may end a rider's participation early, without refund, if they ride unsafely or place the group at risk.
