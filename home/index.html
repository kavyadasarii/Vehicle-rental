About Trail Ridge

Built by riders, run for riders

We started with two ATVs and a borrowed trailer. Today we run a fleet of 40-plus vehicles and a crew of guides who know every rut and river crossing on our trails personally — because they cut most of them.

  • Every vehicle inspected daily Oil, brakes, tires and fuel checked before it ever leaves the yard.
  • No experience needed Every rental starts with a hands-on walkthrough on flat ground first.
  • Local, trail-graded routes Twelve marked trails rated by difficulty so you ride what fits your group.
Trail highlights

Three ways to spend the day

Every trail is graded before you set out, so you can pick a ride that matches your group — from a first-timer's loop to a ridge run that separates the confident from the careful.

FAQ

Good to know before you book

Do I need a license or prior riding experience?
A valid driver's license is required for all rentals. No prior ATV or UTV experience is needed — every rental starts with a guided walkthrough on flat ground before you head out on trail.
What's the minimum age to ride?
Riders must be 18 or older to rent a vehicle solo. Younger riders can ride as a passenger on a UTV with a parent or guardian. Full details are on our Safety & Requirements page.
Can I bring my own helmet or gear?
Yes — you're welcome to bring your own DOT-approved helmet and riding gear. If not, we provide a helmet, goggles and gloves in your size at no extra cost.
What happens if it rains on my booking day?
We'll call you the morning of your slot if trail conditions are unsafe and help you reschedule free of charge — no cancellation fee for weather.
