What's included

Every rental includes

No hidden line items at the trailhead desk — this is what's already in the price you see above.

  • Helmet, goggles & gloves Fitted to your size, or bring your own DOT-approved gear.
  • Full tank of fuel Every vehicle leaves the yard topped up — you return it however you like.
  • Pre-ride walkthrough A guide runs you through the controls on flat ground before you head out.
  • Daily safety inspection Oil, brakes, tires and fuel checked before it ever leaves the yard.
Before you book

What you'll need at the trailhead

Valid license

A driver's license is required for every rental — bring the physical card, not a photo of it.

18 or older

Solo riders must be 18+. Younger riders can ride as a UTV passenger with a parent or guardian.

Closed-toe shoes

Required for every vehicle type — we can't hand over the keys without them.
