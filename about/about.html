About Trail Ridge

Built by riders, run for riders

We started with two ATVs and a borrowed trailer. Today we run a 40-plus vehicle fleet, twelve graded trails and a crew of guides who know every rut and river crossing personally.

  • 40+vehicles in the fleet
  • 12marked trails to ride
  • 4.8 / 5average rider rating
  • 1:6guide-to-rider ratio
Since 2016running trails out of Shamirpet, Hyderabad
Our story

From a borrowed trailer to the trailhead

Trail Ridge began with a simple idea: off-road riding should be easy to start and hard to forget. Our guides cut most of the trails themselves, so they know the terrain better than any route sheet.

Every vehicle is inspected daily, every rental starts with a hands-on walkthrough on flat ground, and every trail is graded by difficulty — so first-timers and seasoned riders can both ride what fits.

Our mission & our vision

Our mission

Make off-road riding safe, simple and unforgettable

We put every rider on a well-inspected vehicle, a graded trail and a guide who makes sure they feel confident — whatever their experience.

  • Daily vehicle inspections, no exceptions
  • A hands-on briefing before every ride
  • Routes matched to your group's skill level
Our vision

The most trusted trailhead in Hyderabad

A place where first-timers become confident riders, and where the trails stay open, well kept and respected for years to come.

  • Riders who return and bring friends
  • Trails maintained by the people who ride them
  • A crew that keeps growing its skills
What we stand for
