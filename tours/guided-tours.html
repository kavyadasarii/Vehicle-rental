Good to know

Questions before you book

Do I need my own gear?

No — helmet, goggles and gloves are included and fitted to you at the trailhead. You're welcome to bring your own DOT-approved helmet if you'd rather ride in your own gear.

Can I book a tour as a private group?

Yes. Switch the price toggle above to "Private group" to see the flat rate for booking an entire tour to yourselves, regardless of how many riders you bring along.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain doesn't stop a tour — the trails drain well. In heavy weather or lightning, your guide will call it and we'll rebook you at no charge for another day.

How far in advance should I book?

Half-day and sunset tours can often be booked the same week. Full Moon Night Rides and the Two-Day Expedition run on a fixed calendar, so book those at least two weeks out.

Is there an age limit?

Riders must be 18 or older to operate a vehicle solo. Younger riders can join as a passenger on a UTV alongside a parent or guardian on select tours.
